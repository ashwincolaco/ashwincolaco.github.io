Making ML and LLM inference cheaper with the ideas behind query optimizers.

My research starts from a simple observation: a cascade of models and a query plan are the same kind of object. Both are an ordering of operators with different costs and different selectivity, and both have a best order that can be found offline. I use cost models, predicate ordering and plan search to run ensembles, route LLM calls and process data streams for less.

I am a Hasso Plattner Institute research fellow. In summer 2026 I was a student researcher on the systems research team at Google, where I built an agentic code generation framework for databases. The systems it produced ran about 9x faster than state-of-the-art in-memory databases on query latency.

I am looking for full-time research scientist and applied scientist roles.
