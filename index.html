ICDE 2026

GenIE

Simulator-driven iterative data exploration. Semantic filters and context-aware cost models for Object-Entity-Relationship databases cut exploration from days to minutes, with 35% lower query latency and 2.5x throughput.

arXiv:2511.12057
ICML 2026

LazyStack

Unifying stacking and cascading for efficient ensemble inference. An MDP solved by value iteration finds the few model orderings that matter, and learned substackers decide when to exit: up to 38x speedup at 97%+ accuracy retention across vision, text, tabular and LLM tasks.

Project page with interactive demo
NeurIPS 2026

NOMAD

Streaming intrusion detection by dynamically building model chains, inspired by predicate ordering in query processing. Reduces false positives by 40%. With UT Dallas and the Army Research Laboratory.

arXiv:2511.00290
SIGMOD 2027

SALUS

Automated auditing of NL-to-SQL benchmarks. Weak supervision over multi-agent output finds the annotation errors that silently distort leaderboard results.
